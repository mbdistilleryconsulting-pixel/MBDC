Who We Are

Specialized Advisory for Ethanol & Commercial Distilleries

MBdistilleryConsulting is a specialized advisory firm that provides technical solutions to improve the operational efficiency and profitability of ethanol and commercial distillery plants. We help plants reach their full potential with a clear focus on delivering results on the ground — not just advice on paper.

Having spent years managing complex plant operations, our founder possesses a deep understanding of what it takes to run a highly efficient and profitable distillery. His expertise lies in micro-level process control — from optimizing starch conversion formulas and maintaining precise water balance charts, to analyzing consecutive 24-hour plant status data for continuous improvement.

Our Core Philosophy

Precision Data + Continuous Monitoring = Higher Profits

We believe a highly profitable distillery is not built only on large machinery, but on precise data, accurate ratios, and continuous monitoring. Even a small process improvement can save lakhs of rupees annually and significantly increase yield.
