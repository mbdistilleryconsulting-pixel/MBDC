Core Process Operations

Mastery across every stage of ethanol production — from grain unloading to Zero Liquid Discharge.

End-to-End Capability

Deep Expertise in Every Critical Section

We understand the interdependencies between sections and optimize the entire process chain for maximum overall yield and efficiency.

01

Unloading & Storage Section

Efficient grain handling, moisture control, and inventory management to protect raw material quality and minimize losses.

02

Milling Section

Mesh size optimization for ideal particle distribution, ensuring maximum surface area for efficient liquefaction and high starch conversion.

03

Liquefaction Section

Jet cooking operations with precise enzyme dosing protocols to achieve complete gelatinization and consistent DE values.

04

Fermentation Section

Yeast propagation systems and contamination control strategies that deliver high alcohol yields with zero contamination incidents.

05

Distillation & MSDH Section

Multi-pressure columns optimization for reduced specific steam consumption while consistently achieving 99.8%+ purity ethanol.

06

Decanter & Dryer Sections

Centrifuge operation excellence and dryer control for production of high-protein DDGS with optimal moisture and nutritional profile.

07

Environment Management

WTP, ETP, and CPU systems engineered and operated for full compliance with Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) norms.

08

Power Plant — Boiler Operations

Coal-to-steam ratio optimization, efficient combustion control, and ash handling systems for reliable and cost-effective steam generation.
